Company Profile :
Musti Musik is an edtech startup that operates in the field of music education. Our mission is to provide quality music education to the masses.
Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled Technical Writer with a background in English or a related field to work with our AI/ML engineers. The ideal candidate will have a strong command of English writing and a passion for transforming complex technical concepts into clear, engaging content. You will play a critical role in documenting AI/ML engineering research, producing progress reports, and writing technical papers.
Responsibilities:
1. Progress Report Writing:
Work with our engineers to develop progress reports that effectively present the status of technical projects, focusing on content, language, organization, and format.
Review and include major report elements such as abstract, introduction, related works, methodology, interim results, project status, future plans, and conclusion.
2. Technical Paper Development:
Write technical papers demonstrating a thorough understanding of discipline-specific structure, format, and language use.
Ensure clarity and coherence in technical writing tailored for both specialist and non-specialist audiences.
Create concise abstracts that capture the essence of technical reports, making complex information accessible to diverse readers.
Present technical information effectively by integrating graphics and text elements to enhance understanding.
3. Justification and Claims:
Make convincing justifications and claims based on real-life disciplinary sources, ensuring the credibility and relevance of the content.
Requirements:
Bachelor’s degree in English, Communication, Engineering, or a related field.
Proven proficiency in English writing, with excellent grammar and style.
Experience in technical writing, preferably in an engineering or scientific context.
Familiarity with AI/ML concepts and terminology is a plus.
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.
Proficient in graphic design tools for integrating visuals into reports.
Experience with academic or research writing.
Familiarity with various formatting styles (APA, IEEE, etc.).
Benefits :
Competitive paid salary
Practical experience in the engineering/writing industry
Remote and flexible work, result-oriented
Opportunity to build a network
Potential for future employment
