PWYW
PAY WHAT YOU WANT PRICING MODEL

### What is PWYW?
It's a pricing model where the customer sets the price, often also called "pay-what-you-can" or "donations."
It can be used to increase accessibility and affordability while allowing supportive customers to pay more.
It conveys confidence in the product's value and empowers the customer to determine the appropriate price.
### Benefits for Online Courses
**Empowers the Customer:** Gives students control over the valuation, leading to a sense of respect and potentially increased engagement.
**Conveys Confidence:** Signals that the creator trusts the value of their course enough to let the customer set the price.
**Increases Accessibility:** Makes the course available to a wider range of individuals, including those with limited budgets.
### When to Use PWYW (Specific Circumstances)
The PWYW model is considered a risky strategy that only works in specific circumstances and should generally **not** be the primary pricing model for your entire business or a flagship course. It is best suited when:
**You Have a Loyal Audience:** Fans or loyal followers are more likely to support the creator by paying a fair price or more.
**You Are Selling a Low-Cost Product:** It works better for low-price points, impulse buys, digital products, or lead magnets, not high-value courses (e.g., $2000).
**You Are Sponsoring a Charitable Cause:** This gives students an added incentive to pay what they feel is right.
**You Are a Non-Profit Organization:** Used to make educational resources more accessible.
**As an Alternative to a Free Trial:** You can offer limited access for a small, student-chosen fee, asking for a small commitment from new students.
### Best Practices
**Set a Minimum Price:** This ensures costs are covered and that students contribute something.
**Suggest a Price:** Providing a reference point, such as "Most people pay XX $," helps customers who are uncertain about what to pay.
**Offer a Fixed Pricing Option:** Pair the PWYW option (often with limited access) with a fixed, full-price option (often with unlimited access).
**Be Transparent:** Explain the costs and why you are using the model to build trust.

Why this model works best?
Protects Revenue
We set Minimum Price 49,000 or 55,000
Affordable enough for christmas
Positioned as underpriced gift
But still gives contributions
Average Revenue Per User ranges 1.3x - 2.2x the minimum price

Creates “I Want To Give More” Sentiment

Donation % must be attractie but not dangerous

Higher than corporate benchmarks (1–10%) → feels generous

High perceived value remains intact
Setting the minimum ensures Musti Musik still feels **premium**, not “cheap.”

# 📌 Ringkasan Singkat (Bahasa Super-Clear untuk Bos)
### ❌ Tanpa Minimum Price → Risiko besar.
ARPU bisa jatuh ke 20–30 ribu.
Revenue turun 70–85% dibanding harga normal.
Walaupun donasinya “terasa besar”, bisnisnya babak belur.
Tidak sustainable untuk Musti Musik.
### ❗Yang akan terjadi kalau tanpa minimum:
Banyak peserta bayar 5K–10K (common in Indonesia PWYW)
Yang bayar mahal tetap ada, tapi minoritas
Kamu tidak bisa prediksi revenue
Donasinya malah kecil → hanya 400K–1 juta
Brand value jatuh (“kok kelas bagus tapi bebas 5 ribu?”)
### ⭐ Kesimpulan final:
PWYW tanpa minimum **tidak cocok untuk Musti Musik** — terlalu berisiko secara finansial dan menurunkan value brand.
