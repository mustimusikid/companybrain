## THE VSL FLYWHEEL PARADIGM SHIFT
Imagine two different restaurant strategies:
**Traditional Approach: **
Stand outside giving free samples (awareness)
Invite interested people for discounted appetizers (consideration)
Hope some stay for a full-price meal (conversion)
Outcome: Half-empty restaurant, high acquisition costs
**Flywheel Approach: **
Prime location where hungry people already walk by
Glass walls showing satisfied customers enjoying meals
Immediate full-price orders from interested customers
Natural attraction of new customers who see others dining
Outcome: Full restaurant, lower acquisition costs, exponential growth
