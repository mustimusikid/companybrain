## 7: Jazz Scale
Berikut adalah scale major, blues, mixolydian, dan bebop in C:
C Major	: C - D - E - F - G - A - B
C Blues	: C - Eb - F - Gb - G - Bb
C Mixolydian	: C - D - E - F - G - A - Bb
C Bebop	: C - D - E - F - G - A - Bb - B

Rumus scale major:
1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 7

Rumus scale blues:
1 - 3b - 4 - 5b - 5 - 7b

Rumus scale mixolydian:
1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 7b

Rumus scale bebop:
1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 7b - 7
