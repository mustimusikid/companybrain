> Alur Customer Success member Akademi Online Musti Musik — onboarding member baru hingga penanganan member menjelang expired, beserta 8 template pesan.

## Flow: Convert (New Member)

1. Buat akun
2. Invite group (pesan 1)
3. Isi presurvey (pesan 2) + Kasih Cheat Sheets (sesuai event)
4. Greeting di Group VIP (pesan 3 dan 4)
5. Announce Onboarding (pesan 5) + Kasih students handbook di group

## Flow: Member Menjelang Expired

1. Reach via mentor (pesan 6)
2. Pengiriman info harga (pesan 7)
3. Lanjut/tidak:
   - **Lanjut:** Pembayaran via mentor + forward ke Ci Dian dan customer support tim tech → Tracking Resubs (di data tiap bulan) di sheets success
   - **Tidak:** Kick dari group setelah expired (pesan 8)
