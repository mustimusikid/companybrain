# Director of Marketing & Communications
LOCATION: MANSFIELD & ALEDO
HOURS: 25 - 40 HOURS PER WEEK
ABOUT US
Destiny Voice and Music Studio is dedicated to providing top-tier voice and music education to students of all ages and skill levels. Our mission is to ignite passion and cultivate confidence in people through *mentoring, training, and by sponsoring moments that matter for a lifetime. *With a team of passionate instructors and a commitment to excellence, we aim to redefine music education and become a leading voice in the industry.
OUR VALUES
 -Because people matter to God, people matter to me.

 -We respond, take initiative, and have a self-employed mentality.
JOB OVERVIEW
As the Director of Marketing at Destiny Voice and Music Studio, you will play a pivotal role in driving the growth and success of our brand. You will be responsible for developing and executing strategic marketing initiatives to increase brand awareness, attract new students, and drive revenue growth. We are looking for a creative and results-driven marketing professional who is passionate about music education and dedicated to making a positive impact in the lives of our students.
RESPONSIBILITIES
**Develop and Implement Marketing Strategy:** Create and execute a comprehensive marketing strategy aligned with the company's goals and objectives. Identify target audiences, market segments, and key messaging to effectively promote our programs and services.
**Brand Management:** Oversee brand development and ensure consistency across all marketing channels. Maintain brand integrity and identity through messaging, visual elements, and brand guidelines.
**Digital Marketing:** Lead digital marketing efforts including website management, SEO, SEM, social media marketing, email marketing, and online advertising. Optimize digital marketing campaigns to increase online visibility, engagement, and conversions.
**Content Creation:** Develop compelling and relevant content for various marketing channels including website, blog, social media, email newsletters, and promotional materials. Collaborate with instructors and students to showcase their talents and achievements, as well as their students’. Highlight community partners and events for cross promotion.
**Customer Acquisition:** Drive customer acquisition initiatives to attract new students and expand our student base. Develop targeted campaigns, promotions, and offers to generate leads and increase enrollment. Write phone and email scripts for membership coordinators to use during sales and enrollment process.
**Public Relations:** Manage public relations activities to enhance brand reputation and visibility. Cultivate relationships with music educators and community partners to establish brand awareness in schools and in the community.
**Marketing Analytics:** Monitor, analyze, and report on marketing performance metrics and KPIs. Use data insights to optimize marketing strategies, improve campaign effectiveness, and drive continuous improvement. Prepare monthly and quarterly reports for CEO with relevant metrics and KPIs.
**Budget Management:** Develop and manage marketing budgets, allocating resources effectively to maximize ROI. Track expenses, manage vendor relationships, and negotiate contracts to ensure cost-effective marketing solutions.

QUALIFICATIONS
Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field
Minimum 5 years of experience in marketing, with at least 2 years in a leadership role.
Proven track record of success in developing and implementing marketing strategies that drive business growth.
Strong understanding of digital marketing channels, techniques, and best practices.
Excellent communication skills with the ability to articulate ideas clearly and persuasively.
Creative thinker with a passion for music education and a commitment to excellence.
Skilled graphic designer with experience working in Canva and/or Photoshop to create all digital and print marketing materials.
Self-starter who can work independently and is very results oriented
Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data, generate insights, and make data-driven decisions.
Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment with a high degree of autonomy.
Lover of people and stories.
Experience planning and directing special events.
Willingness to work evenings and weekends when needed.
Strength in delegating tasks to team members and overseeing results.

BENEFITS
Competitive salary based on experience
Phone allowance ($50 / month)
Technology resources provided – including computer and software subscriptions to ensure success in the position
12 paid holidays per year (Good Friday, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Independence Day, 3 days for Thanksgiving, 5 days for Christmas)
5 paid personal days per year (after 6 months)
Opportunity for advancement and growth in this position and leadership in the company
A dynamic and collaborative work environment with a passionate team
50% off private lessons for yourself and family members

# Director of Marketing & Communications
## KEY RESULTS AREAS

**1. Brand awareness:** Measure the growth in the number of people who are aware of Destiny Studio and our products/services and* track monthly. *Metrics to measure: website traffic, social media engagement, and email subscribers.

**2. Lead generation and conversion: **Track the number of leads generated through marketing campaigns and the conversion rate from leads to customers monthly. This will be measured through website form completions and new lesson/class enrollments.

**3. Customer engagement:** Monitor the level of customer engagement with Destiny Studio’s marketing content and communications. This can be measured through metrics such as social media interactions (likes, comments, mentions, tags, shares), Google reviews, email open and click-through rates, and  blog post shares.

**4. Customer satisfaction and loyalty: **Assess customer satisfaction through surveys, reviews, and feedback. Measure customer loyalty by tracking retention and referrals.  Monitor Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and work to increase CLV annually through cross-selling, upselling, and referrals.

**5. Online presence and reputation:** Monitor your company's online presence and reputation by tracking online reviews, ratings, and mentions. Aim to maintain a positive online reputation and address any negative feedback promptly.
