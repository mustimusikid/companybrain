> Reference material from the Build A Music School course archive.

Hi [Name]

I’m working on my budget for the year ahead and I’m wondering if you can answer a few quick questions for me please?

*(*If you don**’**t have accounting software **… **)*
I would like to implement an online accounting software solution in my business.
Which software do you recommend? Do you get any discounts with particular services?

*(*remember to take into consideration which software actually plugs into your student management system if you have one**… **or which one you should chose if you**’**ll move to an SMS software in the future)*

In my online accounting software, how do I export a report that shows all my cash movements for the month? I need the numbers to include sales tax for cashflow purposes? Is there an easy function to access this quickly or a shortcut I can set up?

Do you have a list of dates where my various tax payments are due so I can put these in my calendar and cashflow forecast? If you could send me the dates and the tax types that would be awesome.

Where can I find my up coming tax payments in my accounting software to best budget what the payments are going to be as they accumulate?

What’s dollar value does an equipment purchase in my business go from being a fully tax deductible expense to needing to be classified as an asset?

Do you think there are any particular business insurances I need to look into this year to stay compliant or mitigate potential risks? Keeping in mind the size of my business and my cashflow, what are your recommendations on weighing up the genuine risk of a situation occurring and me needing the insurance vs the savings of not purchasing said insurance(s)?

I’m looking to simplify my payroll and tax deductions. What payroll systems do you recommend for a business of my size? Do you have any connections for discounts with any particular providers?

What percentage of an employees pay am I obligated pay in superannuation/retirement and health insurance? Are there any other regular fees I should be budgeting for for staff costs?
