> Reference material from the Build A Music School course archive.

Hi <their name>

My name is <your name> and I work for a social enterprise called <your school name>. This year we’ve been lucky enough to have secured funding for an incredible music show we are currently touring around schools in the <your town/city> area.

I’m just ringing to ask who would be the best person at <their schools name> to email with an info pack to see if it’s an opportunity you’d like to offer to your students?
____________

Great, thanks <their name>, can I just read that email back to you to double check it?

[Read back name and email address]

_____________

Fantastic, I’ll get our team to send that through in the next 24hrs.

[Wrap up call]

____________________________________________________________________________

**Note - The magic is in nailing the first two sentances….

**Social enterprise **- If you have a charitable focus, you are one, if you don’t, why the heck haven’t you been listening to me!!!!!!!

**Currently touring**  - implies scarcity, the tour is happening now so they need to act now!

**Funding** - You’re the funding and it’s limited, they don’t need to know any more than that. If you get asked where the funding comes from, you say from some generous donors and through money you raise at <your schools name>

**if it’s an opportunity you’d like to offer to your students** - phrasing it this way means for them to say no makes them sound bad.

**BE CONFIDENT on the phone, relax, and smile as you talk! You got this! BAM!!!**
