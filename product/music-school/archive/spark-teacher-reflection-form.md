*Observer's Name:

**1. Teaching Style:**

-Share your observations about the teacher's teaching style. What did you find engaging or effective?

**2. Interaction with the Student:**

-Describe how the teacher interacted with the student. Did they create a positive and encouraging learning environment?

**3. Explaining New/Difficult Concepts:**

-Comment on how the teacher explained musical concepts. Were the explanations clear and easy to understand?





**Overall Comments:**

-Share any additional thoughts or positive feedback you have about the teacher's performance during the lesson. Highlight any exceptional moments or teaching techniques.







**Recommendations:**

-Based on your observations, are there any recommendations you would give this teacher to continue their success and growth with future lessons?







**Lesson Structure:** 

-How did the teacher begin and end the lesson? Did you feel that the lesson was formatted in an engaging way? Did they include wrapping up the lesson with sending Opus1 notes and asking about the Golden Ticket?
