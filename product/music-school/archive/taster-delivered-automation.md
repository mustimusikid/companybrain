**Taster Delivered Automation**

**CENTRES**** – PRIVATE LESSONS (APPOINTMENTS)**

Once a taster lesson has been delivered and attendance has been marked, the student’s card on the prospect page will automatically be moved to the ‘Centre Taster Delivered’ stage. Any student cards that are in this stage will receive a series of 12 communications from TMW, with a mixture of automatic emails and SMS and manual phone calls.
Customers can reply to TMW comms by phone, email or SMS. If the customer replies and engages at any point and the communication needs to become more manual, simply drag the card out of the ‘Centre Taster Delivered’ stage and into the ‘Centre Manual Follow Up’ stage in the prospect page. The customer will no longer receive the automatic comms and tasks/dates for follow ups can be created manually.
If a customer replies and is not interested in continuing with lessons, mark them as ‘Lost’.
This is the structure of comms:

**DAY 1**

**Automated Conversion Email** - After a student's private taster lesson has been delivered and attendance has been marked, they will automatically move into the 'Centre Taster Delivered' stage of the prospect page and be sent an automated conversion email, which offers them a weekly slot on the same day and time that the taster was delivered. The system will automatically hold this lesson space for 15 days.

**Please note that due to having to build groups manually at present, we are unable to have an automatic conversion email set up for these, as there is no weekly session that the customer can self-enrol into.** These students receive no first email and go straight to the first call.*

**Follow-up email** - If for any reason the automated conversion email mentioned above cannot be sent, this email will send instead. The above email will not send if there is a conflict in the calendar, such as no lessons available due to summer break.

After the initial automated emails listed above on Day 1, we then move into a mixture of manual and automated steps. All manual steps will show in the prospect page as the next task on the student’s card.

The goal of this automation is to have a customer engage in some way. Ultimately, this answer to the question of weekly lessons will be:

Yes
Yes but not now
Let me think about it
No

If a customer engages in any way, we need to remove them from the automatic communications. This is done by simply moving the student’s prospect card from ‘Centre Taster Delivered’ to ‘Centre Manual Follow-Up'. Once moved, they will no longer receive any automatic communication, and you will have to manually update the next task with a sensible date.

If a customer replies and is not interested in continuing with lessons, mark them as ‘Lost’.

If a customer does not engage at all then they will eventually reach day 45 and be moved to ‘Lost Member’, which will trigger a cold leads campaign that will try to win them over with more automated marketing.
