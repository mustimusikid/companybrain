**School show call back script example**

The purpose of this call is to honour your word from the previous email you sent. You said you’d call and so you need to call. If you don’t, you’ll be in an awkward position of never knowing if the email reached them and them just ignoring you and you awkwardly sending follow up emails expecting a different result. That’s what a crappy sales person does, not our #BAMSQUAD ;p

Hi <their name>

This is <your name> calling from <your schools name>, Is <name> available to speak with please?

_________________
If you get a blocking question like **“No they’re unavailable right now”**

Say something like
“No problem at all, we’re just in an email conversation and i thought it would be easier to pick up the phone, but they weren’t expecting my call. Do you know when would be the best time to catch them for 5mins?

*Remember*, you never want to lie (you are in a conversation, they just haven’t replied ;), but you want give off the impression to the administrator/\gatekeeper that the person you’re trying to speak with knows you and would happily take your call if they were there. (Fake it til you make it) ;p

_________________

**When you speak to the person you’re after**

Hi <their name>

This is <your name> calling from <your schools name>, I sent you an email about a free schools tour currently going through the <your city/town> area at the moment, did that email make its way to you ok? I’m hoping it hasn’t ended up in your junk folder?

*squeeze that whole section in before they get a chance to answer.
*Giving them an out with the junk folder line means you don’t come across as attacking them with not responding
*Also, you never want yes/no questions when it’s at the cold call stage, you want open ended question that will keep them on the line

From here, there’s a number of ways the conversation goes……

They’ve seen the email but were too busy to reply
Answer: I totally understand, I’ll give you a call back in a week, but i’ll flick you a follow up email today and confirm some of the available dates we have left on tour, just so you can fire them out to your team to consider as well.

They’ve seen it and aren’t interested?
No problem at all and thanks for at least considering it. Just before you go, obviously money isn’t the issue with funders covering the cost, so was there any particular reason you weren’t interested in this opportunity for the students?
Listen respectively and do your best to try answer any concern

They’re too busy at the moment with too much on
Try find out when would be the best time to book ahead for a concert, maybe you can book one in 2, 4,or 6 months time. You can calendar a date to get in touch again

They are interested… this is the best result and the easiest to deal with
Fantastic, now would you like to book a time on the phone now or would you like to take a few options back to your team and I can come back to you in a few days? (*notice, it’s never a yes/no question)
Goal is to answer any basic questions they have and to get a booking, or a call back for a booking

*With your language you want to always be implying that you’re popular at what you do and this isn’t your first school show. So when they ask a question about a feature of the show, answer with things like, e.g “at most schools we set up 45mins prior to the concert start time.” “the majority of concerts go for 30mins, but we’re available to do up to a 45min show” etc….

**Fake it til you make it!**
**Be confident, you’re awesome and so is your show!!! **

**Final key** - be sure to mention that since the show is free we do ask to be able to give each child a promotional flyer on the way out. This is something I can send to you over email to preview. It just let’s them know about <your school name> and the opportunities available if they are inspired by the show to want to pursue their musical passions.
