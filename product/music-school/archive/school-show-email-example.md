**School show email example**

Hi <Name>

I’ve just spoke with <administrator's name> and <he/she> said you would be the best person to contact about this free opportunity for <school name>.

I work for <your schools name> and we have an amazing educational music show currently on tour through the <your city/town> area; and we’ve been lucky enough to have received enough funding to offer [x] amount of free shows. We would love to offer one of these fully funded shows to your school.

I have attached an information pack for you along with some photos and references.

The shows are educational, highly interactive and very funny. We have a 30-min or 45-min (or whatever times you’d like to offer) options depending on your schedule.  We require access to the hall/gym/performance space at least 45mins prior to the start time and our pack down takes 20mins.

Students will experience:
[List 4-5 of the best highlights from the show and some educational outcomes, e.g hear and learn about musical styles from all over the world]

Again, this is free to the school, the only thing we do ask is that we are able to give students a promotional flyer on the way out of the show. This is something I can send to you over email to preview. It just lets them know about <your school name> and the opportunities available if they are inspired by the show to want to pursue their musical passions. (if you do free trials, then pitch it as a gift to all students rather than promotion - works even better).

We’re on tour for the next couple months (*or whatever time period you want to say) and have a number of available dates in both morning and afternoons. As these are a getting filled quickly it would be best to hear from you or your leadership team as the which potential days/weeks would best suit your school and I can then come back to you asap with some options.

I’ll follow this email up with a phone call in a week if I haven’t heard from you.

Thanks <their name>, look forward to hearing from you soon
