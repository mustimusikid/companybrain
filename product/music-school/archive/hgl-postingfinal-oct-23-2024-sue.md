**Administrative and Sales Coordinator**
**Halifax Guitar Lessons**

Help architect Halifax Guitar Lessons’ future! We are growing and need a responsible and efficient Administrative and Sales Coordinator! The successful candidate will follow up on and close sales opportunities, provide reception, and manage administrative tasks. Halifax Guitar Lessons is a fast-paced, growing small business, looking for someone who can work part-time hours on evenings and Saturdays, and potentially full-time hours with benefits in the future. The office is currently located in Clayton Park and will move to Joseph Howe Ave in the new year.

To be successful in this position you will need:
A diploma in Office Administration from a recognized educational institution or the equivalent combination of education and experience
Two years’ related experience in sales and office administration
Ability to play a musical instrument is considered an asset
One year’s experience using databases
Intermediate level of proficiency using a Mac
Basic level of proficiency in MS Word, Excel, and Outlook
1 year’s experience using Asana, MyMusicStaff, and Zoho are considered assets
Valid NS drivers’ license, or the ability to obtain one, and access to a reliable vehicle is considered an asset.
