**WORKING WITH CHILDREN AND YOUNG PEOPLE QUESTIONNAIRE**

At Goodtime Music Academy we recognise that a large proportion of our work involves interaction with children and young people. We are committed to the well-being of children, young people and their families. This is in accordance with legislation such as the Privacy Act, the Human Rights Act and the Vulnerable Children Act.

All Goodtime workers have a full and active role to play in protecting these individuals. We therefore ask that you give your full attention to filling out this questionnaire to the best of your knowledge.
