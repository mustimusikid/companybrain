**Get ready to rock!** We hope this letter finds you in a fun and energetic mood, just like the one we’re confident you’ll bring to Spark! Your personality is what sets you apart from other candidates and your passion for impacting the lives of kids is exactly what we're looking for in our Spark team. We're thrilled to offer you the position of Front Desk Administrator at Spark School of Music and Dance!

Now before we get too ahead of ourselves, we gotta get through all the nitty gritty details. So buckle up and let's get to it.

**Position**. You will start in a part time position of Front Desk Administrator. By accepting this role, you will have responsibilities that are reasonable and consistent with such a position. You will report directly to Dasha Ruckle.

**Compensation.** In consideration of your services, you will be paid $15 per hour, payable in accordance with the standard payroll practices of the Company and subject to all withholdings and deductions as required by law.

**Employment Relationship.** Employment with the Company is for no specific period of time. Your employment with the Company will be “at will,” meaning that either you or the Company may terminate your employment at any time and for any reason, with or without cause.

**Contingent Offer.** The Company reserves the right to conduct background and/or reference checks on all prospective employees. Your job offer is contingent upon clearance of such background and/or reference checks as applicable. This offer can be withdrawn if any of the above conditions are not satisfied.

As required, by law, your employment with the Company is also contingent upon your providing legal proof of your identity and authorization to work in the United States. This offer, if not accepted, will expire at the close of business on 01/15/2024.

This letter and other accompanying documents supersede and replace any prior understandings or agreements, whether oral, written or implied, between you and the Company regarding the matters described in this letter.

If you accept this offer, we would like to schedule a start date of 01/09/24.

To accept this offer and join the Spark School of Music and Dance family, please sign the attached offer letter by 01/09/2024.
We can't wait to have you on board and have you join the team at Spark!

The Spark School of Music and Dance Team
