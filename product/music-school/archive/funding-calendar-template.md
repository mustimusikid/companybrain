## Sheet 1 - (NAME OF YOUR CHARITA

| (NAME OF YOUR CHARITABLE TRUST) - FUNDING CALENDAR |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FUNDERS NAME | WEBSITE LINK | CRITERIA FOR FUNDING | FUNDING LIMITS | APPLICATIONS OPEN | APPLICATIONS CLOED | APPLICATION MADE | AMOUNT REQUESTED | DATE RECEIVED OR DECLINED | AMOUNT RECEIVED | % RECEIVED | COMMENT |
| Put the name of the fund. e.g the good guy foundation | Put the web link to the fund | Put what they allow you to fund, e.g wages, instruments, vehicles, overheads etc. Each fund allows and doesn’t allow certain things. | Put the amount you can/should apply for. Some funds will say, we fund a maximum of $10,000, but you may be able to see from previous applicants that they typically fund $2000-3000. So it’s best to ... | Put the date applications open | Put the date applications close. | Put the date you made the application | Put the amount you requested in your application | Put the date you heard back - this is important to collect for cashflow purposes for the following year so you know when you’ll hear back form each fund. | Put the amount that you received, even if it was $0. | Put the percentage of the amount you received. e.g if you applied for $10,000 and received $5,000 then it’s 50%. It helps you track how successful you are. | Put any additional comments, this could be feedback from the fund on your application or anything you’ve learned and want to do better next time you apply etc. |
