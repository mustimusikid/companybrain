**Ignite Music ****Lessons**
**Administrat****or and Sales**** Coordinator**

**Summary:** Reporting to the Operations Manager, the Administrative Coordinator is responsible for engaging potential students, providing reception, responding to requests for information, communicating with customers/guardians, maintaining the information in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, and closing customer sales.

**Responsibilities**
Engage potential students by providing information, determining their fit for our services, inviting them to come for a free assessment or introductory lesson, and following up with them to gage their level of interest
Provide reception (in person, emails, and phone calls) by promptly greeting them by name, responding to requests for information, processing payments, and ensuring the reception area is neat and tidy
Enter and maintain data in the CRM by soliciting information from new students, entering and maintaining the student information, and generating reports
Ensure all current leads are followed up by reviewing CRM data/information, contacting perspective students/guardians to gage interest in becoming a student
Communicate with students/guardians by being knowledgeable of the standard operating procedures, generating mass emails from the CRM, providing information regarding individual lesson availability, advising them when a lesson is cancelled, and registering or changing students’ classes
Ensure an adequate supply of office supplies by conducting informal inventories, asking for and recording requests for supplies, researching vendors, and placing orders, receiving,  and storing the supplies
Provide support to teachers by keeping the class calendars accurate and up to date, ensuring classrooms are set up and tidy, printing the most recent students’ lesson notes, and completing other administrative tasks
Maintain the standard operating procedures documentation by reviewing the information, researching, recommending and implementing approved changes, and updating the document
Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations by being knowledgeable of same, managing first aid supplies, and recommending emergency procedures
Perform other related duties as assigned

**Technical Competencies**
A diploma in Office Administration from a recognized educational institution or the equivalent combination of education and experience
Two year’s related experience in sales and office administration
One year’s experience playing a musical instrument
One year’s experience using databases
Intermediate level of proficiency using a Mac
Basic level of proficiency in each MS Word, Excel, and Outlook
1 year’s experience using Asana, MyMusic Staff and/or Zoho are considered assets
Valid NS drivers’ s licence, or the ability to obtain one, and access to a reliable vehicle is considered an asset.

**Typical Behaviours**
**Attention to Detail: **Able to accomplish tasks through concern for all the areas involved, no matter how small.  Proofreads documents to ensure correct grammar, spelling and formatting. Monitors and checks work or information and plans and organizes time and resources efficiently.
**Communication: **Able to write in a variety of communication settings and styles; can get messages across that instigates appropriate actions, including difficult conversations; orally communicates positively in a variety of formal and informal settings; and actively listens.
**Customer Service****:** Is dedicated to meeting the expectations and requirements of customers in a manner that provides satisfaction for the customer and fosters customer loyalty. Is respectful of others, helping or serving them to meet their needs by discovering those needs and figuring out how to best meet them.

**Initiative: **Identifies what needs to be done and confidently takes action to achieve standard of excellence beyond job expectations.
**Planning and Organizing: **Sets priorities, goals, tracking systems and timetables to achieve maximum productivity. Is able to change focus to another task at short notice; able to manage stress that may arise with conflicting priorities.
**Relationship Building: **Able to develop, maintain, and strengthen relationships with others inside or outside of the organization who can provide information, assistance, and support; gains support for ideas and motivates others to advance the objectives of the organization and influence outcomes; understands the global impacts of behavior with peers, students and parents; able to present key points of an argument persuasively, negotiate and convince others.
**Teamwork and Cooperation:** Works cooperatively with others to achieve common goals while demonstrating a positive attitude; open to learning new skills and methods and is able to give and receive feedback.  Keeps other team members up to date.  Addresses conflicts or issues within the team in a positive and open manner.

**Working Conditions**** (October 2024)**

This position is currently part time – approx. 15 -18 hours between the hours of 4pm-8pm Monday through Thursday.  It is expected this will become a full-time position with a mixture of daytime, evenings and weekend hours.

**Physical Demands: **Must be able to:
Sit, use computer monitor, mouse and keyboard, fax, printer and telephone for periods of time with frequent opportunities to stand and move around.
Lift and carry 10 pounds short distances.

**Environmental Conditions: **Must be able to work within, and understand, the small office environment.

**Mental Demands: **Must be able to manage a number of projects/issues at one time, with interruptions and deadlines.
