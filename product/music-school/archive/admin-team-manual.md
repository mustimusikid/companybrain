**Added Core Rules to the handbook**
**Core Rules:**
***Double (and Triple) Check Your Work.*** Before you send an email or text. When putting an appointment on the calendar. So many silly mistakes can be avoided when attention-to-detail and care are exercised. Take a minute to look over previous conversations before reaching out or responding.
***Seek to Understand BEFORE Being Understood.*** With customers or co-workers... get into the other person’s shoes. Think about what THEIR experience will be... of the instruction, of the marketing, of the work. Ask questions. Really listen to what people have to say.
***Be a “Go-Giver.”*** Once you understand someone, you can know how to serve them. How much you influence others is determined by how abundantly you place other people’s interests first. Business growth is determined by how many people you serve and how well you serve them. These principles should govern interactions with everyone that touches the business.
***Write Down Problems.*** Consistent problems can be eliminated if creativity is deployed against them. But, a problem can’t be solved unless it has been “captured” on paper. This is the first step to solving an issue in the business. Write! It! Down!
***Write Down Tasks****.* The beginning of creativity is eliminating distractions from the brain. Don’t allow yourself to go through your workday in a haze of distractions. Write down what needs to be done and then set aside times to get those tasks done.
***Be Differentiated.*** People act out and can be unreasonable. Don’t take this personally. Dissociate yourself from the situation and remain calm in the face of irrational or unreasonable demands. Be clear about where the lines have been drawn... and defend those lines from those who would seek to take advantage. Do this with a smile and humor.
***Demand More From Yourself.*** Don’t take the easy way out. Go the extra mile. If you told someone that you will send them something today... then you do it!
***Demand More From Others***. People are silently begging to be led. They are inspired by confidence and strong leadership. Once you’ve earned someone’s loyalty, use that power to lead them to hard places... in the work, in the instruction, in the cost to be a part of the “club.”
***Ask Questions.*** When working, never hesitate to ask questions from someone who knows... or Google. Don’t reinvent the wheel. It’s shocking how much has already been systematized ... easy answers are on the other side of humility and curiosity.
***Communicate Well.*** Information is giving out; communication is getting through. What’s crystal clear for us is often fuzzy for others. It’s better to over communicate than to under communicate for these reasons: people can’t read your mind, they forget, they get distracted, they haven’t bought into your rationale, and they drift off course. Set your teammates up for success by being proactive with your understanding of a situation and communicating well around it. It’s better to tell someone something they already know than to not tell them something they needed to hear.
