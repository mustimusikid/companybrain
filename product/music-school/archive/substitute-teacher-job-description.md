Are you an awesome musician with a flexible schedule? Do you thrive on spontaneity and making music fun? If so, we want you to join our team as a Substitute Drum Lesson Instructor!
**About Us:**
We're a vibrant, contemporary music school dedicated to spreading the joy of music to students of all ages and skill levels. We believe in mentoring and inspiring kids.And the way we get to do that is through music!
**So What’s The Gig:**
As a Substitute Private Music Lesson Instructor, you'll be the superhero of the music world – swooping in when our regular instructors can't make it. You'll have the chance to teach a variety of instruments. Like Batman…only cooler*Your mission? Inspire kids and be a cool adult that teaches them music!*
**What You'll Do:**
Step into the spotlight when our regular instructors need a break.
Inspire students with your passion for music and your teaching expertise.
Jam out with students of all ages and skill levels.
Create a safe and supportive learning environment.
Share your musical wisdom and enthusiasm.
**What You'll Need:**
A knack for playing and teaching music.
Patience and an infectious love for music.
Flexibility to jump in on short notice.
Reliable transportation.
A can-do attitude and a sense of humor.
**Why Join Us:**
Low Commitment: No need to lock in a long-term schedule. We'll call you when we need you, giving you the freedom to pursue other musical adventures. Plus, if it doesn’t work for your schedule…no problem. We mean it. LOW COMMITMENT.
Build Your Portfolio: Diversify your teaching experience and work with a wide range of students.
Flex Your Creative Muscles: Spark is all about bringing creativity to the forefront, and we encourage you to do just that!
Supportive Team: Join a team that's passionate about music and dedicated to helping you succeed.
Ready to Make & Inspire The Next Generation of Musicians? If you're ready to share your musical ability and make a difference in the lives of kids, hit that apply button and join our team today! Together, we'll create unforgettable musical moments, one lesson at a time.
*We are an equal opportunity employer and welcome candidates of all backgrounds to apply.*
Job Type: Part-time
Pay: $23.00 - $26.00 per hour
Expected hours: 1 – 30 per week
Schedule:
After school
Work setting:
In-person
Work Location: In person
