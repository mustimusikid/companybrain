# Executive Director
The executive director will be responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the performing arts school, including managing staff, developing and implementing policies and procedures, and overseeing the budget. They will work closely with the board of trustees to set the strategic direction of the organization and ensure that it is aligned with the mission and goals. The ideal candidate will have a proven track record of leadership and management experience, as well as strong communication and interpersonal skills. They should also have a deep understanding and passion for the performing arts, individuals with unique abilities, and be committed to providing high-quality education and opportunities to everyone in the community.
## Requirements and skills
Bachelor's degree in a related field (Master's degree preferred).
7-10 years of experience in arts administration, nonprofit management, or a related field.
Strong leadership and management skills, with the ability to motivate staff and volunteers to achieve organizational goals.
Experience in financial management, including budget development and revenue generation.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to represent the organization to external stakeholders.
Demonstrated success in program development and implementation.
Knowledge of the performing arts industry and trends.
Experience working with a Board of Directors.
Strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.
Commitment to the mission and values of the organization.
## Responsibilities
Provide leadership to and manage the efforts of site staff to ensure appropriate support of all departments
Retain a diverse, highly qualified staff and volunteers by providing career coaching, growth, and personal development for workers
Ensure that services and funding relationships are robust enough to meet or exceed strategic goals and objectives
Work with the Board of Trustees to develop and implement a strategic plan that aligns with the school's mission and goals. Monitor progress and adjust strategies as needed to ensure success.
Oversee the school's finances, including budget development, financial forecasting, and fundraising efforts. Ensure financial sustainability through effective management of expenses and revenue generation. This includes managing financial/procurement accounts including Amazon, ShowTix4U, QuickBooks, etc.
Work with the school's Creative Director to improve existing programs and develop and implement innovative programs that align with the school's mission and meet the needs of the community.
Work with the school’s Program Director to hire, train, and manage staff to ensure effective program delivery and operational efficiency. Foster a positive and supportive work environment that promotes staff engagement and retention.
Work closely with the Board of Trustees to ensure alignment between the school's mission, values, and strategic priorities. Provide regular reports to the Board on the school's financial and operational performance.
Represent the organization to external stakeholders, including donors, community partners, and the media. Build and maintain relationships that support the school's mission and goals.
Ensure compliance with all relevant laws and regulations, and implement risk management strategies to protect the school's assets and reputation.
Ensure proper mail distribution.
Maintain the website calendar with tech rehearsals and show dates.
Act as Scholarship Coordinator.
Act as Facilities Coordinator.
Act as Vocational Training Coordinator, working with the Creative and Program Directors as needed.

# Creative Director
The Creative Director is a leadership position responsible for conceptualizing, developing, and executing the overall artistic direction and vision of the organization. The Big Ideas start here. They are responsible for generating creative concepts that align with the organization's goals and vision, and for bringing those concepts to life through various media and channels, such as performances, exhibits, or digital content.

The Creative Director develops and implements the curriculum, designing and supervising the artistic direction of the school, and working with other members of the organization's leadership team to ensure that artistic concepts are aligned with the school's budget and strategic plan. The Creative Director will generally work independently, but still collaborate with other staff members to ensure that their creative concepts are implemented and support the organization's overall goals and objectives.
## Requirements and skills
BSc/BA in advertising, fine art, design or relevant field; MSc/MA will be considered an asset
Proven experience in a related field, such as advertising, marketing, or brand development, and the arts, including hands-on experience in creative process, marketing, graphic design and brand development
Strong creative vision and the ability to generate new ideas for artistic projects or events.
Ability to clearly communicate their artistic vision and direction to other team members, clients, and stakeholders.
Strong leadership skills to manage and inspire a team of artists, writers, or designers.
Ability to align their artistic vision with the organization's goals and budget, and develop strategies to achieve those goals.
Familiarity with relevant software and tools for creating and managing artistic content, such as Google applications, Canva, and social media.
Ability to manage their time effectively to meet deadlines and juggle multiple projects simultaneously.
A keen eye for detail to ensure that all artistic content is of high quality and meets the organization's standards.
Adaptable to changing circumstances, such as tight deadlines or unexpected challenges, and able to adjust their artistic vision accordingly.
## Responsibilities
With the Executive and Program Directors, contribute to formulating the strategic objectives of the organization
With the Executive and Program Directors, initiate and set goals for programs according to the strategic objectives of the organization
With the Executive and Program Directors, plan the programs from start to completion involving budget, deadlines, milestones and processes.
Develop and execute the overall artistic vision and direction of the organization.
Create and develop concepts for artistic projects, performances, or events.
Select shows for group performance classes.
Develop and implement the organization's curriculum.
Collaborate with other members of the organization's leadership team to ensure that artistic concepts are aligned with the organization's budget and strategic plan.
Oversee the creation of artistic content, such as performances, exhibits, or digital content, and ensure that it is of high quality and meets the organization's standards.
Stay up-to-date with industry trends and developments, and use that knowledge to inform the organization's artistic direction.
Manage the organization's artistic assets, such as costumes, sets, and props, delegating to Production Assistants as appropriate.
Manage the organization’s publishing and distribution of original works, coordinating with appropriate personnel regarding technical and legal requirements.
Promote the organization's artistic work and events to the public, and develop strategies to increase visibility and engagement.
Schedule and lead orientation meetings as appropriate (such as auditions, Mainstage, etc.)
Serve as the community performance coordinator (see  workflow).
Serve as the marketing coordinator (see  workflow).
Serve as the grants coordinator (see  workflow).
Work with  on their show needs.
Give building tours to potential students and new teachers.
Serve as Theatre for Youth Coordinator.

# Program Director
The Program Director is responsible for the delivery and overall success of each program. You will supervise department directors and inform management on progress and performance. A successful Program Director must have a broad knowledge of program management principles. They must have a strategic mindset as well as be able to lead and develop their subordinates. The goal is to ensure every program will be delivered successfully and add the highest possible value to the organization.
## Requirements and skills
Proven experience as program director or other managerial position
Thorough understanding of project/program management techniques and methods
Outstanding knowledge of data analysis, reporting and budgeting
Working knowledge of Google drive and associated programs, and program management software (e.g. Asana)
A business acumen with a strategic ability
Excellent organizational and leadership skills
An analytical mindset with great problem-solving abilities
Excellent communication skills
BSc/BA diploma in management or a relevant field; MSc/MA is a plus
## Responsibilities
With the Executive and Creative Directors, contribute to formulating the strategic objectives of the organization
With the Executive and Creative Directors, initiate and set goals for programs according to the strategic objectives of the organization
With the Executive and Creative Directors, plan the programs from start to completion involving budget, deadlines, milestones and processes
Develop budgets for approval by the executive director
Devise evaluation strategies to monitor performance and determine the need for improvements
Supervise all department directors involved to provide feedback and resolve complex problems
Discover ways to enhance efficiency and productivity of procedures and people
Apply change, risk and resource management principles when needed
Read reports prepared by managers to determine progress and issues
Ensure program operations and activities adhere to legal guidelines and internal policies
Keep senior management informed with detailed and accurate reports or presentations
Assign classes and lessons to the appropriate department director for oversight.
Schedule private lessons (see  workflow)
Roll over semester registrations for private lessons
Weekly/every other week parent news emails
Act as volunteer coordinator (see  workflow)
Respond to phone calls, info box and contact us emails, and texts or assign to someone as needed.
Give building tours to potential students and new teachers.
Serve as Costuming Coordinator.

# Office Assistant
The office assistant acts as the ‘face’ of our company and ensures visitors receive a heartwarming welcome. You will also coordinate all front desk activities, including calls, texts, reservations and guest services. You will also ensure our spaces are neat, professional, and in good repair. This is a part-time position not to exceed 16 hours per week.

As an office assistant, you should combine a pleasant personality with a dynamic professional attitude. Our ideal candidate can deal efficiently with complaints and has a solid customer service approach. Ultimately, you should be able to ensure our front desk provides professional and friendly service to our customers.
## Requirements and skills
Proven work experience as a Front desk manager or Reception manager
Hands on experience with office machines (e.g. copymachines and printers)
Thorough knowledge of customer service, office management and basic bookkeeping procedures
Proficiency in English (oral and written)
Solid knowledge of Google drive and associated applications
Excellent communication and people skills
Good organizational and multitasking abilities
Problem-solving skills
High School diploma; additional certification is a plus
## Responsibilities
Ensure our spaces are tidy and have all necessary equipment and material
Ensure timely and accurate customer service
Handle complaints and specific customer requests
Troubleshoot emergencies
Monitor stock and order office supplies
Ensure company’s policies and security requirements are met
Calculate payroll (see  workflow)
Be in the lobby to greet and answer questions
Act as showcase coordinator (see  workflow)

# Department Directors
The department director will be responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the art, music, dance, or theatre department, depending on their specific area of expertise. They will be responsible for contributing to and implementing policies and procedures, as well as managing staff and resources to ensure that the department operates efficiently and effectively. They will work closely with the executive director and other directors to ensure that the organization's overall goals and objectives are being met. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in their area of expertise, as well as demonstrated leadership and management experience. They should also have strong communication and interpersonal skills, and be committed to providing high-quality education and opportunities to our community.
## Requirements and skills
Bachelor's or master's degree in relevant field or related field
Several years of experience in leadership and management
Knowledge of teaching principles and practices in relevant field
Strong communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills; this will include periodic meetings as needed.
Experience working with children and young adults
Ability to develop and manage budgets
Familiarity with relevant laws and regulations, including child protection laws and regulations
Leadership and management skills, with ability to motivate and guide a team
Strong organizational skills, with ability to manage multiple projects and priorities
Familiarity with relevant technology and software
Ability to work collaboratively with other department directors and staff
Knowledge of trends and developments in the relevant field, and ability to stay current with emerging best practices and techniques.
## Responsibilities
Create/maintain a checklist of what content should be in your department’s group class notes and private lesson notes.
Check class notes for completion and content and follow up with the instructor as needed.
Check student lesson notes for completion and content and follow up with the instructor as needed.
Audit classes and lessons periodically, including at least one audit of each lesson/class under your purview within the first four weeks.
Check in with instructors about ordering supplies/music as needed and direct them to the online form
Check in with instructors about song choices, monologue/scene choices (good notes will already have this information). Make sure the choices are age and experience level appropriate.
Ensure instructors have set appropriate and measurable goals with their students and are assisting students with working towards those goals.
Develop a student contract as appropriate for classes under your purview and assist instructors in providing and collecting the contracts (paper versions seem to have more impact on behavior).
Attend showcases. Remind instructors that they are also required to attend all showcases for their students.
# Show Directors

Show directors are assigned by group class and are responsible for staging a show during the mutually-agreed-upon performance run. If there are multiple instructors for one class, one will be noted as the Show Director and shall fulfill or assign these responsibilities.
## Responsibilities
Follow deadlines and processes established by the Creative Director to:
Report props/costumes needs
Request props/costumes ordering
Report set/projection/lights/sound needs
Request music and/or soundtracks
Announce casting
Give parents information on rehearsals, costume needs, hair and makeup requirements, shoes, and show times via both email and in the class notes. You may delegate this to the assistant director but are still responsible for ensuring completion.
Communicate with assistant directors to coordinate tasks and ensure they have what they need.
Work with the Executive Director to make or help make programs.
Coordinate ticket sales with Executive Director, being mindful of presales if necessary to ensure each family gets at least some tickets
Work with the Program Director to obtain volunteers for
Ticket scanning*
Ushering and passing out programs*
Monitoring backstage*
Post-show laundry
Help with painting/building/making sets*
Organize costume prep and maintenance for the show:
Creating/sourcing*/sewing/altering
Steaming/ironing
Organizing temporary storage*
Organizing dressing rooms*
Assistance with hair and makeup*
Work with the Hospitality Coordinator to arrange:
Tech Week dinners/snacks/drinks.
Cast Parties: host/snacks/drinks
Organize props/sets/projection*/lighting/sound needs
Work with the Creative Director to collect cast bios and write a blog post for them
Work with the Executive Director to ensure all necessary dates are on the website calendar.
Attend a pre-production meeting, scheduled in coordination with the Creative Director and key staff, within the first week of rehearsals.
Review scripts and other show material in advance of the semester / rehearsal season start and be prepared to present a "vision" for the show at the pre-production meeting.

*These tasks are appropriate to delegate to a Student Production Assistant.
# Private Lesson and Group Class Instructors

Private lesson and group class instructors are the backbone of this organization. Instructors are assigned lessons and classes in cooperation with MLP leadership.
## Requirements and skills
The ideal candidate has at least a bachelor's degree or is pursuing a degree in the field, OR can demonstrate proficiency (through documented experience and performance). At least one year of experience teaching individuals and/or group classes is required. Some experience in teaching children with special needs is a plus, as is knowledge of assisting students navigate voice changes if you teach voice.
## Responsibilities
Complete training () as assigned by MLP leadership.
Follow deadlines and processes established by the appropriate Department Director.
Maintain notes on each student or class and share them with the parents. (, )
Set semester goals. These goals should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. Some examples of goals could include dates to have a piece memorized, expanding a piano student’s memorized chord library, sight reading, self-taping, etc.
Ensure students have materials they need to rehearse and to perform (tracks, sheet music, theory book, lesson book, props, etc).
