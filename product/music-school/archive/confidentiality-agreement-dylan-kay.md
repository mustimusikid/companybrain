**11.****	Confidentiality****	****	****	****	****	****	****	****	****	**

11.1**	**The Contractor will treat as confidential any information that comes into its possession in the performance of this Contract, whether such information relates to the business, procedures or technical operations of AGS, or the clientele of AGS.

11.2	The Contractor may not, both during the term of this Contract or following its termination, disclose confidential information to any third party without the prior written consent of AGS.

11.3	The Contractor is bound by the matters set out in Schedule 3.

**12. ****	Conflicts of Interest****	****	****	****	****	****	****	****	**

12.1	The Contractor agrees not to enter into Contracts, business interests and/or activities (either directly or indirectly) which may conflict in any way with the interests of AGS and/or the obligations under this Contract.

12.2	It is agreed that under no circumstances will the Music Teaching Contractor signing this contract attempt to divert ‘AGS Customers’ or students away from the School for their own benefit or to teach on a private basis. This clause applies during and subsequent to the termination of this Contract.

**Schedule 3****: ****Confidentiality and Restraint of Trade****	****	****	****	****	**

1.	All information which may be supplied to the Teacher by AGS or of which the Teacher may become aware during the course of the contract is Confidential Information and is the property of AGS. Confidential information includes and is not limited to the following:

AGS’s client list and information, client leads, contracts, and program concepts;
All music teaching and student development plans, marketing information and business contracts entered into by AGS with any other party;
All computer programs, manuscripts and software (in whatever form), know-how, technique, trade secrets, copyright material, procedures, processes, standards, music, lesson materials, trademarks or trade names or similar intellectual property;
All matters of a financial nature including, but not limited to, budgets, business plans, forecasts and assets.

2.	The Teacher will treat the Confidential Information as strictly confidential and will not disclose any of the Confidential Information to anyone unless expressly authorised by AGS.

3. 	The Teacher will take all reasonable steps to protect the Confidential Information and to protect its unauthorised acquisition or use.

4.	The Teacher will not make any copies or records of any of the Confidential Information except as expressly permitted by AGS. The Teacher will, when demanded, return to AGS all materials supplied by AGS, any copies or records of the Confidential Information and any other material relating to the Confidential Information, and shall delete or destroy all Confidential Information, and references thereto from any computers and/or other technological devices located outside AGS.

5.	The Teacher will not use or attempt to use any of the Confidential Information in any manner other than for the purpose of the business of AGS.

6.	It is agreed that during the Contract period and for a period of six (6) months after the termination or expiry of this Contract, and within 5km of AGS, the Teacher shall not, without AGS’s prior written consent, directly or indirectly, as a principal, employee, consultant, partner, contractor, or stockholder, or in any other capacity with, any individual, entity, or business enterprise,

a) engage in direct or indirect competition with AGS,
b) conduct a business of the type or character engaged in by AGS at the time of termination of this Contract, or
(c) develop any projects, products, or services competitive with those of AGS.

7. 	The Contractor agrees that for a period of 12 months following the termination of this contract for whatever reason, they shall not, either personally, or as an employee, consultant or agent for any other entity or employer, seek to solicit or carry out any work of the same nature for any client or customer of AGS, with which the Contractor had any contact or dealings whilst contracted to AGS.

8.	In the event any portion of this clause is viewed as unenforceable by any Authority or Court with jurisdiction to consider such clauses, the clause shall apply as modified by the Authority or the Court, or in the event it is not modified by the Authority or Court, the remainder of this clause and agreement shall continue to be enforceable by the parties.
