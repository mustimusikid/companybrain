# ⭐ 1. Tokopedia DOES allow pre-orders
Tokopedia has a built-in feature called **“Pre-Order / Pengiriman Pre-Order”**.
 You can set your product so that buyers know:
The product is not ready stock yet
Shipping will happen after X days
The expected processing time is longer
This is common for:
Books
Merchandise
Custom items
Production batches
So you’re safe — you can 100% pre-order your book on Tokopedia.

# ⭐ 2. How Tokopedia Pre-Order Works
When you publish your product:
### You can set “Waktu Proses Pre-Order” (Processing Time)
Example:
Pre-order processing time: **7–14 days
**
This tells buyers shipping will start later.
Tokopedia allows up to **30 days processing time** for pre-orders
 (so plenty of room for printing and packaging your book).

# ⭐ 5. How Payment Works
Buyers still pay NOW.
 Tokopedia holds the money until:
You ship the item

So the workflow is perfect for your launch:
They pre-order on Tokopedia

You give them the Masterclass bonus immediately
You ship the book later when it's printed
