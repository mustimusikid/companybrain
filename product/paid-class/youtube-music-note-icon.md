Music Note Icon
The little **music note icon 🎵 next to a YouTube channel name** usually appears for **YouTube Music Artist channels**. This is **not something you can add manually**—it's given by **YouTube automatically** under specific conditions.

### ✅ How to Get the Music Note Icon on YouTube:
That icon indicates that your channel is an **Official Artist Channel (OAC)**.
#### To qualify for an Official Artist Channel, you need to:
**Distribute music via a YouTube partner distributor** (like DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, etc.).
Have a **YouTube channel that you manage personally**.
Have at least **one official music release on YouTube Music**.
Follow YouTube's **channel policies and guidelines**.

### 🔁 Steps to Get the Music Note:
**Distribute your music** through a recognized music distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, etc.).
Link your personal YouTube channel during the distribution process.
After your music is released, request the **Official Artist Channel upgrade**:
Many distributors (e.g., DistroKid) let you **apply for OAC directly** from their dashboard.
YouTube will review and **merge your music topic channel + personal channel** into one Official Artist Channel.
Once approved, your channel name will display the **music note icon 🎵**, and you'll get other perks like:
Music tab
Verified status
Automatic playlists (e.g., “Albums,” “Singles”)

### 📌 Note:
The music note is **only for artists and musicians**—you can't get it if you’re a vlogger or regular content creator.
It may take a **few weeks** for YouTube to approve the request.
