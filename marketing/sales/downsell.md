# 📑 Musti Musik Downsell SOP
### 🎯 Objective
Turn prospects who say *“too expensive / no time / not sure”* into paying customers by offering lower-risk, lower-priced, or feature-adjusted versions of our core products — while positioning the original offer as the **best deal**.

## 1. Academy Downsell Ladder
**Main Offer (Anchor):**
12 Months Full Access → Rp1.999.000
 Includes: 250+ modules, community, certificate, bootcamp, live sessions (Kuliah Piano + Bedah Piano).
**Downsell Path:**
**First Downsell – 6 Months (Value Retained, Price Reduced)
**
Script: *“If 12 months feels heavy, how about we start with 6 months instead? You’ll still get full access to everything, just for a shorter time. That way, you can test your consistency without committing for the whole year.”
*
Price: Rp1.199.000
**Second Downsell – 3 Months (Minimum Package)
**
Script: *“Or maybe just try 3 months first? This is our ‘Minimum Package.’ It’s like dipping your toes in before you decide if you want to continue.”
*
Price: Rp699.000
**Feature Downsell – Content-Only (DIY Version)
**
Remove: Live sessions + certificate
Keep: Video modules + community only
Script: *“If you don’t need live classes right now, we can do a content-only version. You’ll still get 250+ modules and community access, just without the live sessions — so it’s cheaper.”
*
Suggested Price: ~Rp499.000 / 3 months
**Free Orientation → Paid Bootcamp (DIY to Paid)
**
If they still say no: *“How about you join our free orientation session this week? You’ll get a taste of how our learning works. If you like it, we have a one-off Bootcamp you can join without subscription.”
*
Paid Bootcamp Ticket: Rp100–200k

## 2. Music School Downsell Ladder
**Main Offer (Anchor):**
3 Months (12 sessions) → Rp4.000.000
1 Month (4 sessions) → Rp1.500.000
**Downsell Path:**
**First Downsell – 2 Sessions Trial Pack
**
Script: *“If 1 month (4 sessions) feels too much right now, how about just 2 sessions first? This way you can experience the learning style with less commitment.”
*
Suggested Price: ~Rp850.000
**Second Downsell – Group Session (Lower Price per Student)
**
Script: *“If 1-on-1 is a bit heavy, we also have small group sessions. You’ll still follow our structured curriculum with a trusted mentor, but the price per session is much lighter.”
*
Suggested Price: ~Rp400–500k per student / month
**Third Downsell – Paid Trial (Single Session)
**
Script: *“We also offer a single paid trial class. Just Rp250.000, and you’ll know right away if this method works for you.”
*
**Feature Downsell – Shorter Duration (30 min instead of 60)
**
Script: *“If time is the issue, we also have shorter 30-minute lessons. You’ll still get structured guidance, but in a compact format that’s easier to fit into your schedule.”
*
